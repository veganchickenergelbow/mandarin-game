Every scene has 4-5 turns, 2 twists, patient/hurried variants, 4 options per turn (exactly one fit), a rung-2 intent, warm-up items and word lists. Existing tests are extended to the new files.

Also in v0.2:
- **Home:** a Week 1 strip of 5 day cards. Each day opens with a skill card of tappable function phrases, then Warm-up, Work and Life. The next day unlocks when today is done (playing ahead is allowed). After Day 5, a "Week 1 complete" card, and replays use new twists.
- **Trust hearts (0-5) for each character:** fit +1 progress, blunt or cave -1, 5 progress = 1 heart. A Characters screen. At 3+ hearts, the hurried mood is the default.
- **Spaced review (SM-2-lite):** intervals 1/3/7/14 days, and wrong resets to 1. Warm-ups take up to 3 due items. Home shows "N words due".
